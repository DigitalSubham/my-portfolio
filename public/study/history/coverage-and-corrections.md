# Study pack: coverage and important corrections

Updated 16 October 2026.

## What is included

- One complete 24-chapter Hindi notes document, with 417 English meaning annotations across chapters. PDF and HTML contain the same study text.
- An English-only bank of 880 questions; 31–48 per chapter. All questions use the requested JSON schema.
- Levels: 1 = easy, 2 = moderate, 3 = hard. These are editorial difficulty labels, not official BPSC classifications.
- Scope: Bihar history and the Indian National Movement. This is not the full GS or Computer Science syllabus.

## Actual exam format differs from the requested practice schema

The JSON follows your requested five-choice format: A–C substantive options, D = More than one of the above, E = None of the above. Advertisement 15/2026 uses A–D answer choices and E to intentionally skip. Do not transfer JSON D/E meanings to the real OMR. Wrong answers or completely unmarked questions attract one-third negative marking under that advertisement; follow any later official instructions.

## Supplied MCQ reconciliation

The source inventory contains 142 Indian National Movement items and 139 Bihar items. Of these, 200 have mapped practice questions; six are unscored because the premise, wording, options or date is uncertain. The other 75 concern geography, environment, state symbols, current office-holders or other GS outside this history pack. They are explicitly marked outside scope, not counted as covered.

BPSC_Source_MCQ_Coverage.json lists the original page and number, extracted English stem, status, mapped question IDs and corrections. Mapping means adapted or topic-covered, not necessarily a verbatim reproduction. Repeated source questions can map to one revised question.
